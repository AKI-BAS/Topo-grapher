5. Data accuracy — please read this one

Topo-grapher sources elevation data from public government datasets, which vary in resolution and age by country and region — from 0.4m LiDAR in some areas down to 30m global fallback data elsewhere (full breakdown on the data coverage page). We do the best conversion we can with what's publicly available, but:

  • This is not a substitute for a licensed land survey. It's a starting point for design, not a certified as-built record.
  • Government source data can be outdated, especially where terrain has since been altered (regrading, new roads, construction).
  • You're responsible for having a qualified professional verify the data against your site before relying on it for construction, drainage, grading, or any decision where being wrong has real cost or safety consequences.
